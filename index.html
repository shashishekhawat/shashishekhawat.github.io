I work on machines that have to keep running on a factory floor, and on the models that tell us when they will not. My research joins digital twins with computer vision and control for prognostics and health management (PHM), so a production system can sense its own condition, diagnose a fault, and estimate how much life is left in it. I am based in the Digital Twin of an Autonomous Factory Lab in the Manufacturing Engineering Section at IIT Madras.

I am open to: collaborations on digital twins and condition monitoring of production equipment, fault diagnosis and remaining-useful-life datasets and benchmarks, and conversations with industry teams working on factory automation and predictive maintenance. Email is the fastest way to reach me.

Research

Digital twins and prognostics & health management

Simulation-linked models of machines and production assets, used for condition monitoring, fault diagnosis, and remaining-useful-life estimation. This is the core of my PhD work in the Digital Twin of an Autonomous Factory Lab at IIT Madras.

Robotics, automation, and control

Sensing, actuation, and control for automated systems — the machine side of the digital twin, from instrumentation and measurement to the design of the hardware being modelled.

Computer vision and data-driven modelling

Vision pipelines and learned models that hold up under real illumination, clutter, and sensor noise — object detection and inspection, and signal- and image-based methods for extracting structure from measurement data.
